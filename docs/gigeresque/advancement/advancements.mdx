# Advancements

This guide explains all advancements in the **Gigeresque** mod and how to unlock them.

## **Within These Twisted Halls...**
- **Title**: Enter the unknown
- **Description**: Locate a Xenomorph dungeon in the world.
- **How to Get**: Travel to and enter any Gigeresque dungeon.

---

## **Yeah, Like That's Ever Gonna Happen**
- **Title**: Yeah, Like That's Ever Gonna Happen
- **Description**: Fail to exploit black fluid blood
- **How to Get**: Try and put black fluid blood in a glass bottle.

---

## **Yeah, Like That's Ever Gonna Happen**
- **Title**: Yeah, Like That's Ever Gonna Happen
- **Description**: Fail to exploit the unknown
- **How to Get**: True to bucket an Xenomorph.

---

## **...We Are Not Alone**
- **Title**: ...We Are Not Alone
- **Description**: Have the unknown enter you
- **How to Get**: Let a Facehugger attach itself to you.

---

## **All I Am is Me**
- **Title**: All I Am is Me
- **Description**: Prevent total genetic collapse by consuming wither suspicious stew and a golden apple.
- **How to Get**: Consuming wither suspicious stew and a golden apple when you have the DNA Disintegration effect.

---

## **Cut It Out**
- **Title**: Cut It Out
- **Description**: Remove a parasite before it removes you.
- **How to Get**: Use the **Surgery Kit** on yourself or another entity.

---

## **Now You're Really In the Spit**
- **Title**: Now You're Really In the Spit
- **Description**: Have two things go horribly wrong at the same time.
- **How to Get**: Acquire the effects Impregnation and DNA Disintegration simultaneously.

---

## **Something Followed Us Back**
- **Title**: Something Followed Us Back
- **Description**: Realise you're no longer safe
- **How to Get**: Trigger the first spawn resulting after opening the Dungeon seal.

---

## **Yeah, Like That's Ever Gonna Happen**
- **Title**: Yeah, Like That's Ever Gonna Happen
- **Description**: Fail to exploit acid
- **How to Get**: Try to bottle acid.

---

## **This Has to Be On Spore-puse**
- **Title**: This Has to Be On Spore-puse
- **Description**: Have three separate infections at once.
- **How to Get**: Acquire the following effects:
  - Impregnation
  - Mycosis
  - DNA Disintegration
