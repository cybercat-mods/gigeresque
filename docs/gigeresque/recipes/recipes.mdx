# How to craft the Surgery Kit

You can craft the **Surgery Kit** using a shapeless crafting recipe. Here are the required items:

<img src="https://i.imgur.com/jiHz7Iu.png" alt="surgery_kit"/>

## Ingredients:
- **Shears**
- **String**
- **Iron Ingot**
- **Golden Apple**
- **Wool** (any color)
